# EP02 — Spider-Man 2: stopping the train (v2 reaction script)

**Movie:** *Spider-Man 2 (2004)*  
**Scene:** After Doc Ock wrecks the controls, Spidey braces his feet on the front of the runaway train, then webs both sides of the street and holds on until it stops at the edge of the broken track. He passes out and the passengers carry him back.  
**Timestamps:** Roughly **1:32:00–1:35:00** of the ~127 min theatrical cut. PLAY timestamps below are approximate and must be confirmed against the rip.

## Numbers (off-screen, checked; 2 spoken numbers)

- Assumptions (deliberately gentle): 4 cars × ~40 t loaded = **160 t**, **100 km/h** (27.8 m/s), stopped over **~300 m**.
- **"About ten tons on each arm"**. KE = ½·160,000·27.8² ≈ 62 MJ. Over 300 m that's 62 MJ ÷ 300 m ≈ 206 kN ≈ 21 tonnes-force, or ≈ **10.5 t-f per arm** with two web lines.
- **"Twenty deadlift world records"**. The deadlift record is ~0.5 t (501–505 kg), and 10.5 ÷ 0.5 ≈ 21, so "twenty".
- Webs (not a spoken number): real spider silk breaks at ~1 GPa. 103 kN ÷ 1 GPa = 1.0 cm², so a line about **1.1 cm across** holds it. The film's web lines look at least that thick, so "real spider silk this thick could hold that" is fair.
- "Can't make rent" is the actual plot of the movie (he gets evicted-level warnings from his landlord, Mr. Ditkovich).
