## Numbers (off-screen, checked; 2 spoken numbers)

- Vault: **~10 t** (generous round number), steel sliding on asphalt with friction μ ≈ 0.5.
- **"About five tons of pull"**: 10 t × 0.5 = 5 tonnes-force (49 kN) just to keep it sliding.
- **"Under a ton"** per car: a Charger is ~1,900 kg with ~48% on the driven rear wheels (≈ 912 kg). On grippy tyres (μ ≈ 0.95) that's ≈ 8.5 kN ≈ **0.87 t-f** before wheelspin. Two cars ≈ 1.7 t-f vs 5 t-f needed, so they'd need ~3× the grip. Hence "lots of smoke, zero vault".
- Damage (not a spoken number): 10 t at 100 km/h ≈ 3.9 MJ of kinetic energy, so "absolutely wrecks those cop cars" is fair.
