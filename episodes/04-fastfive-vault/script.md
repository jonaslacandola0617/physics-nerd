## Numbers (checked off-screen, never shown as citations)
- Vault: **~10 t** (a generous round number for a bank vault that size)
- Steel sliding on asphalt: friction coefficient **~0.5**, so **~5 tonnes-force (49 kN)** just to keep it moving
- Per car: **~2.5 t** of pull needed
- Tire grip limit: a Charger is ~1,900 kg with ~48% on the driven rear wheels. On grippy tires (μ ≈ 0.95) that's **≈ 8.5 kN ≈ 0.87 t per car**
- Shortfall: 49 kN needed vs ~17 kN available, so **~2.9×** more grip than they have
- Power at 100 km/h: 49 kN × 27.8 m/s ≈ 1.36 MW ≈ **1,800 hp**. Two Chargers have **~900 hp** combined
- Kinetic energy of the vault at 100 km/h ≈ 3.9 MJ, so the "it wrecks everything it hits" part is fair
