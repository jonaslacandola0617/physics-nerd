EP26: Speed (1994) — Jumping the 50-Foot Highway Gap
Upload Title: How Did The Speed Bus Jump A Flat Highway Gap? 💀
Top Banner: PHYSICS NERD REACTS TO SPEED
Scene Type: Iconic
Hook Type: Dry Engineering Roast
Sequence: Flat-Road Drop Audit (No ramp to 4-foot gravity drop to Secret real-life ramp)
Fact Notes for Claude: A 15-ton city bus approaches a 50-foot gap on a flat elevated highway at ~67–70 mph (~100 ft/s). Crossing 50 ft takes ~0.5 seconds, during which gravity drops the bus ~4 feet (0.5 * 32.2 * 0.5^2)—and because the front wheels leave the road first while the rear wheels are still supported, the heavy front end immediately pitches nose-down. Without an upward ramp, the bus slams straight into the concrete ledge of the opposite side. In real life, the stunt bus used a hidden launch ramp and the highway gap itself was added digitally in post.
