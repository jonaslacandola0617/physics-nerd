## The angle
Everyone assumes Cap is holding up a whole helicopter. He isn't: the rotor carries the helicopter's own weight. The impossible part is actually **his bent arm**, because of the lever. Correcting a common assumption like this does well in comments.

## Numbers (checked off-screen, never shown as citations)
- A light twin-engine helicopter of this size: **~3 t**
- Cap only fights the **excess thrust**, the part beyond hovering. Assume ~25% extra at full power, so **~750 kg-force**
- 750 kg ≈ **10 people** at 75 kg
- Arm bent at 90°: the biceps attaches ~4–5 cm from the elbow, and the hand is ~35 cm out, so roughly an **8:1 lever**
- Biceps tendon load ≈ 750 × 8 ≈ **6 tonnes-force**
- A heavy 50 kg curl puts ~400 kg on that tendon, so call it **"under half a ton"**
